**Skills Note Document**

**Job Titles and Seniority Levels**
*   **Posting 1:** Manufacturing AI & Automation Engineer – Mid-to-Senior Level (5-10 years of experience)
*   **Posting 2:** Data Engineer I – Entry Level (0-2 years of experience)

**Skills from Posting 1: Manufacturing AI & Automation Engineer**
*   **Required Skills:**
    *   Translating manufacturing challenges into practical automation solutions.
    *   Managing technical projects in fast-paced manufacturing environments.
    *   Cross-functional collaboration (Engineering, Operations, Quality).
    *   Strong written and verbal communication.
    *   Analytical skills and data-driven problem solving.
*   **Preferred Skills:**
    *   Lean manufacturing, Six Sigma, or continuous improvement methodologies.
    *   Deploying AI solutions within enterprise environments.
    *   Experience in semiconductor, cleanroom, or precision manufacturing.

**Skills from Posting 2: Data Engineer I**
*   **Required Skills:**
    *   Proficiency in Python and SQL.
    *   Familiarity with relational data, schemas, joins, and data transformations.
    *   Knowledge of ETL processes and structured data formats.
    *   Basic software development practices (version control, code review).
    *   Written and verbal communication.
*   **Preferred Skills:**
    *   Azure Databricks environment experience.
    *   Implementation of Spark and Delta Lake.
    *   Creating data transformations using medallion architecture patterns (bronze, silver, gold).

**Shared Skills Across Both Postings**
*   Analytical thinking and data-centric problem-solving.
*   Strong written and verbal communication.
*   Cross-functional teamwork and collaboration. 
*   Solid foundation in engineering or computer science principles.

**Reflection**
Despite the disparity in seniority and industry focus between these two roles, both heavily emphasize communication and cross-functional collaboration alongside core technical competencies. It is clear that while specific tools like Azure Databricks or AI automation platforms differ, foundational skills in analytical problem-solving, structured data management, and the ability to convey technical concepts to operational teams are universally demanded in modern engineering and data science roles.
